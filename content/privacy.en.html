This policy explains, without unnecessary jargon, what information may be processed when you visit this site or use any of my projects, where it is stored, who can see it and what you can do about it. The underlying idea is simple: the site is designed to work with as little data as possible, and most information stays on your own device.

1. Controller and scope

The controller of the site is the owner of “Jaime Gaming”, who can be contacted at jaimegamingpro@gmail.com. This policy applies to the website, the documentation and the projects linked from it, unless a project has its own policy or uses a third-party platform with its own rules.

I do not maintain my own server with visitor databases. The website is static and published through GitHub Pages; projects that store information do so, by default, in the browser or on the device of whoever uses them.

2. What this site does not do

The site has no user accounts, registration forms or visitor profiles. I do not use analytics tools, heat maps, advertising pixels or tag managers. I do not sell, rent or transfer data to advertisers. I do not ask for your name, phone number, postal address or payment details to browse.

The site does not set its own tracking cookies. Some embedded third-party content, such as the status panel, may use its own cookies or technical storage; I explain this below so there are no surprises.

3. Technical data when browsing

Like any web page, loading it involves exchanging essential technical data: the IP address, browser and operating system type, the page requested and the time of the request. That data reaches the hosting provider, GitHub Pages, which processes it under its own policy and may retain security and availability logs. I do not access those logs or use them to identify visitors.

The site also requests public data from various APIs to display projects, contributions and status. Those requests are made directly from your browser, so the relevant providers receive your IP address and technical connection data, although I do not send them your identity. Typefaces are served from this same domain: they are not loaded from an external provider.

4. Storage in your browser

To make the site work better, I store information in your browser's localStorage, not on a server. You can delete it at any time from your browser settings; doing so loses the language preference and the cache, but the site keeps working.

The keys I use are, approximately: lang, to remember whether you prefer Spanish or English; gh-cache-v3-…, a copy of the repositories and public profile so they can be shown if the API fails; gh-repo-v2-…, data from a specific project you have opened; and contributions-v1-…, the contribution calendar and summaries for specific days. These copies expire or are replaced when they are updated.

I do not use these keys to identify you, I do not share them with third parties, and they contain no credentials.

5. How the projects handle data

The projects are designed to work, by default, with information stored in your browser or on your device. I do not maintain a server with your data or a usage database. If a project needs to save something, it does so in local storage or in a file on your device, and you can delete it from there.

Some projects are programs that are downloaded and run locally. They may store settings or credentials in files on your device; those credentials do not pass through the website or a server of mine. If the program connects to an external provider that you configure, the content you send is transmitted to that provider, which processes it under its policy and may apply its own costs.

Other features are optional and only activate when you use them: for example, connecting two devices, looking up a file's metadata or resolving a request through an external service. In those cases the provider receives the technical data needed to deliver the feature, including the IP address. If you do not activate the feature, nothing is sent.

Versions marked as tests or prototypes run on your device and do not include their own telemetry, unless their description expressly states otherwise. If a specific project publishes its own privacy policy, that policy prevails for that project. This section describes the common approach for all projects, with no exceptions based on what they are about.

6. Email

If you write to jaimegamingpro@gmail.com, I will process your address, your name if you provide it, and the content of the message in order to reply, assess your proposal or handle an issue. Email is managed through Gmail, a Google service, which applies its own terms and security measures.

I keep messages for as long as necessary for that conversation and do not disclose them to third parties except where legally required. I do not use your address to send newsletters or advertising. The button to copy the address works in your browser and sends nothing to any server.

7. Third-party services

To display up-to-date content, the site queries these services directly from your browser. All of them receive technical connection data, such as the IP address and browser type, and apply their own policies:

  • GitHub (api.github.com, github.com, raw.githubusercontent.com): repositories, profile, READMEs and site hosting. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Lanyard (api.lanyard.rest): the public Discord status of the site owner, not of the visitor. It is an independent community service.
  • github-contributions-api (github-contributions-api.jogruber.de): public contribution calendar.
  • incident.io (statuspage.incident.io): status panel and its API, loaded on the /status/ page.

Links to YouTube, X, Discord and other sites only contact those services when you decide to open them; this site does not preload them. If you block third-party requests with an extension, some parts of the page — such as the projects or the status panel — may not display.

8. Cookies and embedded content

This site does not set its own cookies and does not use analytics or advertising cookies. The status page offers a panel served by incident.io that does not load until you press the corresponding button. Once loaded, incident.io may use cookies or technical storage to remember preferences or keep the panel session; that processing is the provider's responsibility and I have no access to that information. If you prefer to avoid it, do not press the button or block embedded content in your browser; the summary and the external link will remain available without loading anything from that provider.

9. International transfers

Some of the providers mentioned may process data outside the European Economic Area. Where they do, they may rely on their own safeguards, such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses. I do not control those mechanisms and cannot modify them; if you need details, check each provider's policy. Data that stays in your browser or on your device is not transferred to any country.

10. Retention

Information stored in your browser or on your device remains there until you delete it, the cache expires or the site replaces it. Emails are kept for as long as necessary to handle the conversation. Hosting technical logs are governed by GitHub's policy and I keep no copies of my own. I do not build visitor histories or behavioural databases.

11. Security

The site is served over HTTPS and does not receive sensitive data. The exposure surface is kept to a minimum because it is a static site with no accounts or forms. Even so, no system is completely secure: if you detect a vulnerability, write to me before disclosing it and give me a reasonable time to fix it.

12. Your rights

Processing is carried out in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Spanish Organic Law 3/2018 on the Protection of Personal Data and the Guarantee of Digital Rights (LOPDGDD). It is based on the legitimate interest in keeping the site secure and replying to those who contact me, and on your consent when you write voluntarily.

You may request access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability of the personal data I process, and withdraw your consent where you have given it. To exercise these rights, write to jaimegamingpro@gmail.com stating which right you are requesting. I will reply within the periods established by law.

Because much of the information never reaches a server of mine, exercising some rights will consist of explaining how to delete the data from your own browser or device. If you believe I have not handled your request properly, you may lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD, aepd.es).

13. Minors

The site is not specifically aimed at children under 14. In Spain, the age at which a person can consent to the processing of their own data is 14. If you are a minor, use the projects with the supervision of your guardians and do not send personal data without their consent. Tools that run commands or send information to external providers require enough judgement to understand their consequences.

14. Changes to this policy

I may update this policy if the services used, the projects or applicable law change. The date at the top shows the latest revision. Relevant changes will be reflected on this same page and, where they substantially affect processing, will also be announced on the blog or in the relevant repository.

15. Contact

If you have any questions about privacy, write to jaimegamingpro@gmail.com or use the contact page.
